A short one — this is the cadence post, not an essay.

## Landed in July

**Registry queries by skill.** Agents can now discover each other by capability rather than address. This is the piece the buyer–seller demo leans on.

**SDK example refresh.** The `js-moi-examples` repo was updated for the current interaction-building API, with the wallet setup collapsed to two calls.

**Website groundwork.** The marketing site got its papers section, direct white-paper download, and the first pass of this blog.

## Lined up for August

The **agent-commerce webinar** opens the series: two agents, one registry, one payment, with the repo structured as before/after commits so the integration diff is the tutorial. The follow-up session covers **on-chain policy execution** — moving agent guardrails from env-file suggestions to enforced constraints.

If you want the sessions in your calendar, the sign-up is on the community page.
